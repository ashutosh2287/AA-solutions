## Overview

KL Boox House is a private study library. The public site explains the space —
private cabins, four daily shifts across 57 seats, and the facilities students
care about — while a separate login gives students and staff the tools to manage
day-to-day operations.

That split is the interesting part: the marketing site has to convince a student
to visit, and the management system has to make the front desk's job easier.

### What the product covers

- A public site covering private cabins, shifts and facilities
- A photo gallery and location details
- Student login for seat and shift information
- Admin tools for seats, shifts and renewals
- Membership management
