## Overview

fastQ is a campus food-ordering product. Students pick their campus and canteen,
browse the menu by dish, add items to a cart and place an order ahead of a break,
so they spend less time standing in a queue.

The product also includes a loyalty tier called **FastQ Gold**, a help centre, and
clear terms and privacy pages — details that are easy to skip but matter once real
students are using the app.

### What the product covers

- Campus selection, then canteen selection within that campus
- A menu browsed by dish rather than by canteen alone
- Cart and order placement
- Order history and current order status
- A student profile
- FastQ Gold, a loyalty tier for regular users
- A help centre with support and policy pages
